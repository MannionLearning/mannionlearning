FAQ,s

What services do you offer?
We offer a variety of services please see our home page for a full list of these services
Do you offer student discounts?
Yes, we pride ourselves on offering affordable hair cuts for students! When booking online enter your student ID in the notes and make sure you bring the corresponding card on the day!
What are your opening hours?
Monday to Friday 9am to 7pm and 10am to 5pm on Saturdays, we are closed on Sundays.
How can I book an appointment?
You can book an appointment by calling us on 01223 222222, emailing us atsales@Ilbarbiere.co.uk or using our online booking form on the Prices and Bookings page.
Do you provide hair care products?
Yes we offer a range of high-quality hair care products that you can purchase in-store or online.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept cash, major credit or debit cards and contactless payments
Can I bring my own hair products for the barber to use?
Unfortunaly with the rise of allergies and skin conditions in modern times we are only insured to use our own products, but we're sure we will have something that works for you!
What should I do if I need to cancell or reschedule my appointment?
If you need to cancell or reschedule plesase give us as much notice as possible so we can offer your slot to someone else, ideally 24 hours minimum, you can do this by calling, or emailing us.
Is your Barbers wheelchair accessible?
Yes, please let us know if you have any further requirements that we can assist you with.
