About US

Il Barbiere was founded when Pablo Russo came to Cambridge to study business, he had very little money and found no places to get an affordable hair cut.

Luckily for him he met a kind retired barber called John King who cut his hair for a cheap price. This inspired Pablo to open a barber shop affordable

to students and he spent the next 4 years waiting tables whilst studying to save a depoist for a premises which is still at the same location today where

he cuts hair for students affordably passing on the same kindness that John showed him all those years ago!
